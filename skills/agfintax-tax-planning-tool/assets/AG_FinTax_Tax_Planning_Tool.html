S-Corp Election Strategy
S-Corp Est. Annual SE Tax Savings
Eligible: Schedule C / single-member LLC profit > $40,000 (2026 projected)
—
—
—
—
—
📌 As a sole prop you pay 15.3% SE tax on ALL profit. S-Corp pays FICA only on reasonable salary — remaining profit is taken as a distribution, not subject to SE/FICA. Salary defaults to 25% of net profit as a starting point only — the IRS sets no fixed percentage; salary must be supportable as reasonable compensation for the owner's role (industry data, hours, duties). Low salaries are a top S-Corp audit issue — adjust before presenting.
ℹ️ S-Corp election is modeled only for Schedule C / single-member LLC businesses with combined 2026 net profit above $40,000. Businesses already taxed as S-Corp, C-Corp or Partnership are excluded.

Real Estate Capital Gains
📌 Real Estate LT Capital Gains Tax: Federal rate 23.8% (20% LTCG + 3.8% NIIT for high earners) + your state rate. Depreciation recapture taxed at 25% federal. Total effective rate can reach 30%+ depending on state.
Opportunity Zone Investment Strategy
Opportunity Zone Est. Tax Benefit
Defer & potentially eliminate CG tax — invest within 180 days of gain event
—
—
📌 QOZ Funds: Invest CG proceeds within 180 days. Defer original gain tax; hold 10+ years → ALL appreciation in the QOZ fund is TAX-FREE. Applies to both stock and real estate gains. The figure shown is tax deferred, not eliminated.
⚠️ Timing: Under the original OZ rules, deferred gains are recognized on 12/31/2026 — a 2026 investment gets little or no deferral. The OBBBA made OZs permanent with a rolling 5-year deferral for investments made on or after 1/1/2027. Confirm timing with the CPA before presenting.
